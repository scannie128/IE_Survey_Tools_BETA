Calculation method
The page uses forecast temperature, dew point, and surface pressure. Dew point determines water-vapor pressure, then dry-air and water-vapor components are combined to calculate moist-air density.
That density is converted to the equivalent altitude in the International Standard Atmosphere. Pressure altitude is shown separately.
The Worker supplies model-grid surface pressure. When project and model elevations differ, v0.0.1 adjusts pressure hydrostatically to the entered project elevation before calculating density altitude.
⚠ Operational notice
This page provides forecast-based planning estimates, not observed field conditions or flight authorization. Forecast uncertainty generally increases with forecast horizon.
Confirm official aviation weather, aircraft documentation, payload and fuel configuration, terrain, winds, and onsite conditions before operation.
Days 8 through 16 are an extended outlook for trend awareness.
