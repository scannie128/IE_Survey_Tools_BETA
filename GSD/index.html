Calculation details
GSD = (AGL × sensor width) ÷ (focal length × image width)
Results are nominal planning values. Actual processed GSD and usable accuracy can vary with terrain relief, camera calibration, focus, image scaling, aircraft attitude, shutter settings, motion blur, processing choices, and flight-height variation.
Planning reminder: This calculator does not determine whether a flight is legal, safe, or adequate for a required mapping accuracy. Verify terrain clearance, airspace, aircraft limits, ground control, overlap, and project-specific accuracy requirements separately.
